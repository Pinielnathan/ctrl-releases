# CTRL downloads

CTRL lets any of your devices control any other: your phone drives your PC, your PC drives your tablet, and files go across in seconds. This repository only holds the downloads. Everything else is at **[ctrl.chitemere.co.zw](https://ctrl.chitemere.co.zw)**.

| Download | For |
| --- | --- |
| [CTRL-Windows-Setup.exe](https://github.com/Pinielnathan/ctrl-releases/releases/latest/download/CTRL-Windows-Setup.exe) | Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit |
| [CTRL-Android.apk](https://github.com/Pinielnathan/ctrl-releases/releases/latest/download/CTRL-Android.apk) | Android 10 and newer (nearly every phone and tablet) |
| [CTRL-Android-32bit.apk](https://github.com/Pinielnathan/ctrl-releases/releases/latest/download/CTRL-Android-32bit.apk) | Older Android phones that run a 32-bit system |

iPhone, iPad and Mac need nothing installed: open [app.ctrl.chitemere.co.zw](https://app.ctrl.chitemere.co.zw) in the browser to control your other devices.

## Installing

- **Windows** may say "Windows protected your PC" because CTRL is new. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Android** asks once before your browser can install an app: tap **Settings**, allow it, then **Install**.

## Checking a download

Every release lists the SHA-256 of each file. On Windows, `certutil -hashfile CTRL-Windows-Setup.exe SHA256` prints it; it should match.
